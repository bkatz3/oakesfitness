Meet the Concord Team

The coaches who'll be in your corner every session.

Mike Oakes

Owner | Strength & Performance Coach

Mike Oakes is the owner and founder of Oakes Fitness and has dedicated more than 25 years to helping people become stronger, healthier, and more confident through intelligent, personalized coaching.

Read full bio

Since opening Oakes Fitness, Mike has built a reputation for delivering results by combining evidence-based training with a coaching style centered on education, accountability, and genuine relationships.

Throughout his career, Mike has worked with clients of every age and ability—from beginners taking their first steps into fitness to athletes competing at the highest levels. His experience includes coaching Olympic athletes, Division I athletes, professional fighters, bodybuilders, powerlifters, obstacle course competitors, youth athletes, and adults looking to move better, lose weight, reduce pain, and improve their overall quality of life.

Mike's own athletic background reflects his passion for pushing physical and mental limits. He is a former professional mixed martial artist, collegiate wrestler, competitive bodybuilder, powerlifter, and obstacle course athlete. He has also competed on American Ninja Warrior, giving him firsthand experience in strength, mobility, endurance, coordination, and performance under pressure. This diverse athletic background allows him to understand the unique demands placed on athletes while also helping everyday clients achieve lasting success.

What truly sets Mike apart is his coaching philosophy. He believes that movement is medicine and that every client deserves a program built specifically for their goals, abilities, and lifestyle—not a one-size-fits-all workout. His focus extends beyond simply making clients stronger; he strives to improve mobility, posture, balance, joint health, and confidence so clients can continue doing the things they love for years to come.

As an educator and mentor, Mike is passionate about developing the next generation of fitness professionals. He continually studies biomechanics, corrective exercise, strength and conditioning, and human movement while mentoring trainers to provide the highest level of coaching throughout Oakes Fitness.

Whether you're looking to lose weight, build muscle, recover from injury, improve athletic performance, or simply feel your best, Mike is committed to helping you reach your goals through expert coaching, proven programming, and unwavering support every step of the way.

"Movement is medicine. Get stronger, move better, and live with confidence."

Johnny Kuettner

General Manager | ISSA Master Trainer

Johnny brings over 25 years of experience to training, working with clients of all levels and populations to help them achieve their fitness goals.

Read full bio

B.S. Framingham State University. ISSA Master Trainer; ISSA Specialist Sports Conditioning, ISSA Specialist Post Rehabilitative Exercise, ISSA Specialist Fitness Nutrition, ISSA Specialist Fitness Older Adults, ISSA Youth Fitness Trainer.

Johnny's philosophy involves equal importance in strength/resistance training, cardio endurance, mobility, and healthy eating. The most rewarding aspect of the job is seeing the client's hard work pay off and making fitness part of their daily routine.

Johnny played on the Framingham State University Football team as a Defensive Back all four years and was named to the New England Football Conference All-Academic Team all four years. Johnny won the prestigious Scholar Athlete Award his senior year.

Outside of working out, Johnny enjoys traveling, hiking, kayaking, and spending time with his wife Anh, daughter Asia and son Kai, as well as his two dachshunds Oscar and Dieter and greyhound Barney.

Christian

Personal Trainer

Christian is a NASM Certified Personal Trainer with over three years of hands-on coaching experience helping clients achieve lasting results through individualized training.

Read full bio

Christian believes there is no one-size-fits-all approach to fitness. Every person has unique goals, movement patterns, strengths, and challenges, which is why he designs programs tailored specifically to each client.

As a dedicated bodybuilder, Christian has a deep appreciation for the science of strength training, muscle development, nutrition, and proper recovery. His own commitment to bodybuilding has taught him the value of discipline, consistency, and attention to detail—principles he brings into every training session.

Over the past three years, he's helped clients ranging from complete beginners to intermediate lifters lose body fat, build lean muscle, increase functional strength, improve confidence, and develop sustainable lifestyle habits. Whether the goal is weight loss, improving athletic performance, building muscle, or simply feeling healthier and stronger, Christian is committed to helping clients reach it safely and effectively.

One of his greatest passions is continuing to learn—he constantly studies the latest research, training methods, and coaching techniques to provide evidence-based programming and the highest level of support. His goal is to create an encouraging, motivating environment where every client feels supported, challenged, and empowered to become the strongest version of themselves.

Evan

Personal Trainer

Evan holds a Bachelor's degree in Exercise and Sports Science with a focus on clinical exercise science, and is a Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) through the NSCA.

Read full bio

Evan is a former college athlete with experience in professional-level training in sports performance. He specializes in helping clients understand the anatomy and physiology behind movements, and is always willing to educate clients when asked questions.

Evan guides clients in ways to improve overall fitness—through losing weight, cultivating lean muscle mass, gaining strength, increasing mobility and balance, as well as improving overall cardiovascular health.

Evan is a Type 1 Diabetic and has experienced the struggles of living with an autoimmune disease. His own personal experience has motivated him to help others improve their overall quality of life through physical training.

When Evan isn't in the gym training clients, he enjoys spending time with his family, lifting weights in the gym, drumming, and playing soccer year-round.

Joe Garcia

Fitness Trainer

Joe is an ACE Certified Personal Trainer with advanced certifications in Sports Performance, Orthopedic Exercise, and Senior Fitness.

Read full bio

He is passionate about continuing his education and regularly attends hands-on seminars through respected organizations such as Functional Range Conditioning (FRC), Kabuki Strength, and other industry leaders to stay at the forefront of strength, mobility, and performance training.

Joe specializes in helping clients build functional strength, improve mobility, increase stability, and move with greater confidence. His coaching philosophy is centered around creating resilient bodies by strengthening every link in the kinetic chain—from grip strength and foot stability to core control and full-body movement. He believes that improving balance, coordination, and mobility not only enhances athletic performance but also reduces injury risk and improves quality of life for people of all ages.

Whether working with beginners, older adults, or experienced lifters, Joe designs personalized programs that help clients become stronger, move better, and feel more capable in everyday life. His calm, encouraging coaching style helps clients build confidence while safely progressing toward their individual goals.

Outside the studio, Joe enjoys powerlifting, skateboarding, barefoot hiking, and spending quality time with his family.
