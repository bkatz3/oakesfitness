# Oakes Fitness blog writing skill

Write for adults 50+ who want intelligent strength training, not gym-bro content. Studio is Oakes Fitness in Westford and Concord, MA. Founded by Mike Oakes. Belief: movement is medicine. Posts are signed The Oakes Fitness Team.

Read this file fresh from `main` every run. Do not edit this file.

Gold-standard recent post: `blog/2026-08-23-what-is-a-deload-week-and-do-you-need-one-after-50.md`

## 1. Voice

- Direct, adult, calm. Myth first, then the correction. No throat-clearing.
- Open by naming the common belief in one or two sentences, then say what it actually is. Example pattern: "A deload week gets confused with skipping the gym because you're worn out. It isn't that."
- Write like a coach who has read the paper, not like a marketer summarizing a paper.
- Second person ("you"). No "we at Oakes believe" sermons. Studio shows up in a soft CTA and the footer, not the thesis.
- After-50 is the default frame. Do not write a generic fitness article and sprinkle "after 50" on the title.
- Short paragraphs. Question-shaped H2s. Tables when comparing signals or options.
- Internal-link related posts with natural anchor text, not "click here."
- No medical advice. Do not diagnose, prescribe, or tell anyone to stop a medication. Point at training decisions and when to see a clinician.

## 2. Banned words and tics

Never use: hack, unlock, secret, trick, torch, shred, crush, booty, toned, tone up, miracle, detox, superfood, game-changer, anti-aging (as a product pitch), biohack, optimize your life, "just" as a minimizer for hard work.

Never use emoji in the post body, title, or PR title.
Never use exclamation points except in a quoted client line (rare; do not invent quotes).
Never invent a client story. If you need a human example, keep it generic ("a client in his early 60s") only when an existing published post already uses that device, and do not fabricate outcomes.

## 3. Tiers

Pick one. Put it in the PR title as `(Tier N)`.

**Tier 1 — Local / commercial.** Westford, Concord, Acton, Chelmsford, or surrounding towns in the title or lede. Answers a buying question (trainer vs gym, trainer vs PT, what to expect, cost, small group vs 1-on-1). Evidence still has to be real if you cite any. CTA can be more direct. Category examples: local commercial topics already in `scripts/TOPIC_IDEAS.md` under "Local / Commercial (Tier 1)."

**Tier 2 — Educational / evidence.** The default. Sleep, recovery, hormones, nutrition, muscle, bone, brain, injury, cardio, posture, supplements. Named studies with PMIDs or DOIs. Key Takeaways required. Soft CTA only.

**Tier 3 — Practitioner / studio craft.** Coaching craft and mindset (the existing "Hard Pills" and "Chameleon Coach" posts). First-person studio experience is allowed. Still no fabricated citations. No fake 30-year war stories.

If the topic is in the Local / Commercial section of TOPIC_IDEAS.md, it is Tier 1. Otherwise default to Tier 2 unless the topic is clearly about coaching craft.

## 4. Evidence rules

- Citations must be real and checkable. Prefer PubMed (PMID) or a DOI. Link them. Include first author, year, journal, and PMID or DOI the way the deload post does: `([Rogerson et al., 2024, *Sports Medicine - Open*, PMID 38499934](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38499934/))`.
- Before the PR opens, actually verify each study exists (PubMed or the DOI resolver). If you cannot verify it, you may not cite it.
- If a claim needs a study and you cannot find a real one: reframe as practitioner experience in one sentence, or cut the claim. A fabricated citation is the worst failure available to you.
- Report what the paper actually measured (population, n, design) in brief. Do not upgrade a survey into a trial or a trial into a law of nature.
- Do not cite secondary blog roundups as if they were the study.
- Internal links count as navigation, not evidence.

## 5. Structure

Default Tier 2 skeleton:

1. YAML frontmatter (see §6)
2. H1 that matches `title` exactly
3. `*By The Oakes Fitness Team*`
4. Opening: 2–4 sentences. Myth, correction, why it matters after 50.
5. Three to six H2s, mostly questions.
6. Optional table if you are comparing signals or options.
7. A practical "how to" section with numbers a reader can use this week.
8. `## Key Takeaways` — 5–7 bullets. Each bullet is a complete claim, not a teaser.
9. One soft CTA. Prefer the free 360° body audit at `/contact.html`. Do not hard-sell packages or prices unless Tier 1 and the topic is cost.
10. Horizontal rule, then the footer:

```
**Oakes Fitness** | Westford, MA | oakesfitness.com
*Serving Westford, Chelmsford, Littleton, Groton, Acton, and surrounding communities.*
```

Tier 1 may drop Key Takeaways if the piece is a local guide, but keep the footer. Tier 3 may drop the study block if there is nothing real to cite.

Length: roughly 900–1600 words. Do not pad.

## 6. Output format

One Markdown file in `blog/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` (today's date, lowercase slug, match existing filenames).

Frontmatter, exactly these keys, no extras unless an existing post in that category already uses them:

```yaml
---
title: "The Title As A Question Or Claim"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
author: "The Oakes Fitness Team"
category: "Sleep & Recovery"
---
```

`category` must be one of the pillar names already used in `scripts/TOPIC_IDEAS.md` (Sleep & Recovery, Hormones & Physiology, Nutrition Science, Muscle & Aging, Bone & Connective Tissue, Brain & Mental Health, Injury & Joint Health, Cardio & Conditioning, Local / Commercial, Posture & Mobility, Supplements, Habits & Mindset) or a category already used on an existing post in `blog/`.

Do not write `.html`. Do not run `scripts/build_blog.py`. Markdown only.

Mark the topic ✅ Written (YYYY-MM-DD) in `scripts/TOPIC_IDEAS.md` in the same PR.

If no 🟢 topics remain: add 5 new ones under the existing pillars, same numbered style, then write the first of those five.

## 7. Pillars for new topics

When generating topics, stay inside: Sleep & Recovery, Hormones & Physiology, Nutrition Science, Muscle & Aging, Bone & Connective Tissue, Brain & Mental Health, Injury & Joint Health, Cardio & Conditioning, Posture & Mobility, Supplements, Habits & Mindset, Local / Commercial (Tier 1, Westford/Concord/Acton/Chelmsford). After-50 angle required except for studio-craft Tier 3.

## 8. Silent checklist (every item must pass before the PR opens)

If any item fails, fix it. Do not ship.

- [ ] Filename is `blog/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md` with today's date
- [ ] Frontmatter has title, date, author, category; title matches H1
- [ ] Author is The Oakes Fitness Team
- [ ] Tier is chosen and in the PR title as `Blog: [post title] (Tier N)`
- [ ] Opening names the myth and corrects it in the first three sentences
- [ ] No banned words, no emoji, no invented client results
- [ ] Every named study has a live PMID or DOI that was verified this run
- [ ] Unverifiable claims were reframed or cut, not cited
- [ ] At least one internal link to a related existing post (unless this is among the first posts in a new cluster)
- [ ] Key Takeaways present for Tier 2 (5–7 complete bullets)
- [ ] Soft CTA + standard footer
- [ ] Topic marked written in `scripts/TOPIC_IDEAS.md`
- [ ] No `.html`, no builder run, no edits to site code, config, or this skill file
- [ ] PR body contains the full post (title, meta, category, tier, complete body) so it can be proofread in the GitHub mobile app without opening the diff
- [ ] One PR today; if today's blog PR already exists, do nothing
